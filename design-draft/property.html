Overview

The Jefferson model, in Williamsburg.

Motivated seller. A lovingly maintained Jefferson model in the Williamsburg neighborhood of On Top of the World. Just over 2,000 square feet, with three bedrooms, two baths, and a dedicated office/flex room — no carpet anywhere.

The kitchen is finished with generous counter and cabinet space, a breakfast nook, and a separate dining room ideal for entertaining. Take in golf-course views from the Florida room, and start your mornings with coffee in the private courtyard. The functional two-car garage adds epoxy floors and a dedicated golf-cart bay.

The HOA has the home scheduled for fresh exterior paint in August, and the seller will let the new owner choose the colors.
